About Val d'Isère

Val d'Isère is one of the most famous ski resorts in the French Alps and the perfect destination for an unforgettable student ski trip. Together with Tignes, it forms the vast Espace Killy ski area, offering over 300km of pistes for every ability level - from complete beginners to experienced skiers and snowboarders. With stunning mountain scenery, reliable snow conditions, and a lively village atmosphere, Val d'Isère delivers the ultimate mix of adventure, nightlife, and alpine charm.

Après-Ski & Activities

Bars & Nightlife

After a day on the mountain, Val d'Isère comes alive with legendary après-ski and nightlife. Meet friends for drinks, enjoy live music, and experience some of the Alps' most famous venues including La Folie Douce, Coco Rico, IMA, Le Petit Danois, and Underground Café. Whether you're looking for a relaxed evening or a late-night party, there's something for everyone. – perfect for enjoying drinks with fellow students.

Dining

From traditional Savoyard favourites like fondue and raclette to burgers, pizzas, and international cuisine, Val d'Isère has plenty of options to refuel after skiing. The village is packed with cafés, restaurants, bakeries, and takeaway spots suitable for every budget.

Off-Slope Activities

The fun doesn't stop when the lifts close. Students can enjoy ice skating, swimming and spa facilities, sledging, winter hiking or snowshoeing. Perfect for rest days or anyone wanting a break from the slopes.

Practical Info

Transport

Bus transport to and from the destination is included in the price. Departures from both Lyngby and Ballerup Campus. Remember your passport!

Luggage: One large soft bag (not hardcase!), one small bag for inside the bus, a bag with boots and a bag with ski/snowboard.

You can choose to fly for an extra fee or organize transport yourself for a price reduction.

Après-Ski

At La Folie Douce, enjoy cold drinks with fellow students and the guide team. You can also take the gondola to La Folie Douce both up and down. If you are looking for a show of entertainment, then go to Coco Rico where they play with fire!
